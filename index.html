Do you deliver outside Islamabad?

Islamabad and Rawalpindi are same-day or next-day. Other cities go by courier — message us your city and we will confirm the rate.

Is the honey pure?

Yes — pure honey, no sugar syrup and no added flavour. Because it is raw and natural it can crystallise in winter; that is normal and safe.

How should I store the jar?

Keep it closed at room temperature, away from sunlight. Always use a dry spoon so the nuts stay crunchy.

How long does a jar last?

Best within 6 months of packing. The packing date is printed on the label.
